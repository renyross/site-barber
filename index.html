Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer walk-ins?

Walk-ins may be available depending on the day. We recommend calling ahead at +1 (631) 381-9441 or booking online to guarantee your spot.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, we offer kids cuts for younger clients in a relaxed, welcoming environment.

Do you offer fades?

Yes, fades are one of our specialties, from low and mid fades to skin fades.

Do you work with dreadlocks?

Yes, our team works with locs and dreadlocks at every stage, from starter locs to long-term maintenance.

Do you offer starter locs?

Yes, we start new locs and guide you through the early stages of the loc journey.

Can I book a retwist?

Yes, retwist appointments can be booked online or by calling the shop.

Do you offer loc extensions?

Yes, we offer loc extensions to add length or thickness to existing locs.

Do you offer braids?

Yes, braiding services are available. Let us know the style you have in mind when booking.

Do you provide women's haircuts?

Yes, we welcome men, women and kids for haircuts and styling.

Where is the barbershop located?

Bab Nazareth Barbershop is located at 2118 Deer Park Avenue, Deer Park, NY 11729, in Suffolk County.
