A Neighborhood Barbershop

Bab Nazareth Barbershop is a premier neighborhood barbershop located at 2118 Deer Park Avenue in Deer Park, NY, proudly serving clients throughout Suffolk County and Long Island. We welcome men, women and kids of all ages, offering a complete spectrum of grooming services ranging from classic haircuts and razor-sharp fades to comprehensive dreadlock maintenance and protective braid styling.

Our core philosophy centers on uncompromising precision, consistency, and scalp health: flawless skin fades, sharp beard contours, nurtured locs, and styles that maintain their definition well between visits. Every appointment is a personalized consultation designed around your hair texture, lifestyle, and unique aesthetic.

  • Precision Fades, Tapers & Beard Sculpting Expert
  • Starter Locs, Retwist & Healthy Scalp Care Specialty
  • Custom Braids, Twists & Women's Haircuts Full Care
  • Relaxed, Community-Focused Atmosphere All Ages
