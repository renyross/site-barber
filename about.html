About Bab Nazareth Barbershop

Our Community

A Neighborhood Barbershop

Bab Nazareth Barbershop is located at 2118 Deer Park Avenue in Deer Park, NY, proudly serving clients across Suffolk County. We style men, women, and kids with complete grooming services, from classic cuts and sharp fades to loc care and custom braids.

Our focus is precision and consistency: immaculate skin fades, clean beard lines, healthy loc maintenance, and styles that stay sharp between visits in a relaxed, welcoming shop for everyone.
