About Bab Nazareth Barbershop

Your local barbershop on Deer Park Avenue, serving Deer Park and Suffolk County.

Our Community

A Neighborhood Barbershop

Bab Nazareth Barbershop is a barbershop located at 2118 Deer Park Avenue in Deer Park, NY, serving clients throughout Suffolk County. We work with men, women and kids, offering everything from regular haircuts and fades to complete loc care and braiding.

Our focus stays on precision and consistency — clean fades, sharp beard lines, healthy locs and styles that hold up between visits. Whatever brings you in, our goal is the same: quality work in a shop that feels like part of the community.
